How EduStream works

Simple by design, from first click to last lesson.

No lecture halls, no clutter. Four steps, all at your own pace.

Choose from programming, design or business. Each category is organised so you can find the skill you actually need — not just the most popular one.

Tutors sequence their videos into playlists, so every lesson builds on the one before it. You always know what comes next.

Lessons are kept short and focused. Watch one now, pause, rewind, or come back tomorrow — your progress is always waiting for you.

Stuck on something? Ask in a moderated discussion space alongside other learners and the tutor who made the lesson.
