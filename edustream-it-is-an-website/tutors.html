How tutors teach here

Same format, whoever's teaching.

Every EduStream tutor follows three rules, so a course always feels familiar — whatever the subject.

01

Keep it short

One idea per video. Lessons are cut down until only the useful part is left — no hour-long recordings.

02

Put it in order

Every lesson sits in a structured playlist, so you always know what to watch next and why.

03

Stay in the room

Tutors take part in the moderated discussion under their lessons, so questions get answered by the person who taught it.
